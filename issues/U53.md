# U53 Prove "status" does not touch running work

**Model** Claude Haiku 5.5 medium. **Wave** 6. **Timebox** 75 min. **Port** 8853.

## Why

The owner's rule: while a worker runs, a human "what is the status" is answered by the lead from worker state and must not steer, stop, or restart the worker. A note meant for the work reaches the worker only through `steer_work`. This needs a deterministic proof with fake agents (AGENTS.md: team and loop behavior is tested with fake agents first, no tokens).

Read: `apps/server/src/agent/dev-fake.ts`, `apps/cli/src/verify/suites/workers.ts`, `mid-run.ts`, `core/agent/src/mid-run.ts`, `core/agent/src/workers.ts`.

## Do

Extend `apps/cli/src/verify/suites/workers.ts` (or the closest fake-agent suite) with a case:
1. The human asks for slow work; the fake lead calls `start_work`; the fake worker runs several slow steps.
2. Mid-work the human sends "what is the status?". Assert: the lead replies; its reply input contained the worker's live state; the worker conversation received no new input; the worker was not aborted or restarted; the worker finishes and its report reaches the human.
3. Then the human sends a note; the fake lead calls `steer_work`; assert the worker received exactly that input once.

If the fake agent cannot script `start_work` or `steer_work`, extend `dev-fake.ts` minimally. If the assertion reveals the rule is broken, stop and report with the evidence (STATUS blocked, QUESTION) rather than changing product behavior.

## Acceptance

- Run the suite on your own server: `bun yuki verify workers --url http://localhost:8853`, paste the result. Show it fails when you temporarily make the lead's status reply steer the worker (revert after).
- `bun scripts/check.ts` green after merging `main`.
