# U52 round 5: every human message ends in an outcome the human sees

Source: review A (`issues/review/A.md`) A1, A2, A3, A5, A6, A8, A14. Round 4 already fixed A7 (order) and A10 (frame measure).

## Decisions (the coordinator's)

1. **A1.** Route on the whole text. Store the mention tags of the full text on the pending row at send time; routing uses them. Test: 20,000 characters with `@ada` at the end reaches ada.
2. **A2 and A3.** Keep a human refusal notice, one per envelope (the existing `refused:<envelope>:<slug>` key), worded without asking the human to resend: say the message could not reach the agent and is kept in the chat. Then bound the entry for real: cap the bubble in JSON bytes and size the board so brief plus bubble fit; prove it with the worst case (`"\u001b".repeat(16000)`, board at cap, 3 members with 400-character roles). The notice stays as a defensive outcome even when the bound holds. Test that a two-envelope too-large delivery posts two notices.
3. **A5.** A held over-limit burst must not spin: the limit path returns `held` and `flush` does not `retry(0)`. Test: 101 pending messages, turn not ok, last wakeup is `resetAt`.
4. **A6.** Held rows do not count as pending, so initiative wakes and agent mail are not blocked while a burst waits for the reset. Test: held burst, agent mail to its recipient still drains.
5. **A8.** `stop` keeps human messages still in the burst window; they deliver after the stop. Test it.
6. **A14.** Move `fileIdOf`, `bubbleOf`, the whole-text constants and `writeWhole` into `apps/server/src/room/whole-text.ts` with direct tests; delete the orphan R2 object when a claim is refused after the write.
