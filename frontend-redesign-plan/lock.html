2. Four night skies

Each sky is the background for onboarding and for the sidebar top. They're infinite: no ground, no hills, nothing sitting on the bottom edge. Snow falls in three depths that loop forever, and everything else is light.

  • Quiet snow: main's black, snow in three depths, and the azure accent breathing up from below.
  • Aurora: slow azure, violet and teal ribbons across the top, with faint curtain lines.
  • Nightly: t3code's indigo sky, with twinkling stars and sparkles fading into black.
  • Moonlit: a soft moon behind Yuki and a beam of light. The snow only glints where the light catches it.

My pick: Moonlit for onboarding, because the light puts Yuki on stage. In the sidebar it becomes a moon in the corner with snow catching its light. Quiet snow is the safe second: the calmest, and the closest to main.

3. Onboarding

These are main's steps and copy, hosted build:

  1. "Welcome to Kiko" with "Get started".
  2. Sign in through Clerk's modal, which opens by itself as it does on main.
  3. Cloudflare, then "Installing Kiko. This takes up to a minute."
  4. Provider, with main's five choices.
  5. Model: "Kiko does not pick for you".

The frame changes; the flow doesn't. One centered column, the stepper on top, and a glass panel with no border. Yuki is on the sky above it:

  • blinks;
  • leans in when you hover a button;
  • hides behind a leaf while Clerk is open;
  • bobs to the music in its headphones while Kiko installs;
  • perks up when a provider connects;
  • hops when you start chatting.

Then the sky shrinks toward the sidebar's top band and the app fades in. Switch skies with the buttons at the top.
