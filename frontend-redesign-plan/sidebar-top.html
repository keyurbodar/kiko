The sidebar top, with B4

This is the band in place, at the real sizes: the sidebar is 280px by default and resizes between 220 and 420. The sky is the top of the onboarding scene, cropped and dissolved into the sidebar, so the header and search sit on the aurora instead of above a drawn panel. No border under it, no card, no line.

What changes, what stays

Stays exactly as main

Header height 50px, 12px left padding, 8px right. The mark is 22px, the name 14.5px at 600. Two 32px icon buttons. Search field 34px, 8px radius, filled with white at 4.5%. Pin and chat rows 36px, 8px radius. The sidebar itself stays #0a0a0a.

Changes

Main's blueprint art (the grid, the dashed orbits, the planet, the little pulsing dot) is deleted. In its place is the aurora band. The mark becomes R8, and the wordmark image is replaced by the word set in the interface font, so it stays crisp at every zoom.

Why the band, not a card

Main and refs/t3code both separate surfaces by tone, never by lines. A band that fades out is the same idea at the top of the sidebar: one continuous surface, no edge to see. A panel with a border or a rounded card would be the PR's habit, and it is the thing you asked me to stop.

Cost

Static markup, no JS, no image. It paints in the first frame from the snapshot, and the three animations are the only moving parts, all off under reduced motion.
